FAQ

Questions franchisees are asking.

What is Businesses in ChatGPT?

It is OpenAI's new program, launched in beta on November 10, 2026, that lets a business claim and verify its profile in ChatGPT, keep its details current, and see data on how often it appears. The dashboard is called Business Hub.

Can't I do this myself for free?

Yes. Claiming a profile costs nothing, and some owners will prefer to manage their own. This service is for owners who would rather not learn another dashboard, keep up with a beta that is changing quickly, or remember to update one more listing every time hours change.

Will this make ChatGPT recommend my school more often?

Not by itself. OpenAI says claiming a profile does not increase recommendations. What it does is put accurate information in front of ChatGPT and show you how often your location appears. Accurate, consistent information is the foundation the rest of your visibility is built on.

Does my location get its own agent?

Not yet. OpenAI has limited Business Agents to eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses and has not given a date for local businesses. Agent setup and upkeep are included in the Managed plan from the day they become available to your location, at no added cost.

I own several locations. How does that work?

OpenAI requires each location to be claimed separately, so each one is enrolled and billed on its own. Enter the number of locations on the form and we will collect the details for the others by email.

Who owns the profile?

You do. The claim is made in your name and Soulcraft is added as a manager. If you cancel, you remove our access and keep the profile exactly as it stands.

What if my location cannot be claimed yet?

OpenAI currently lets local businesses claim only an existing US listing, and it reviews every claim. If your location cannot be claimed, we tell you, pause billing, and try again when eligibility changes.

What about Claude, Gemini, and other AI platforms?

If another platform opens business profiles or agents to local businesses, managing yours there is part of this service. We will let you know before we add one.

How does billing work?

$50 per location, invoiced monthly by email. There is no setup fee and no contract. Cancel any time by replying to any of our emails.
