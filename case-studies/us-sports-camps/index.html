The method

Rather than guess at keywords, we mapped what AI systems were already researching about the brand. Google Search Console exposes AI-generated sub-queries as impressions with near-zero clicks. Clustered, those sub-queries are a map of the questions engines are asking on a buyer's behalf and failing to find a good answer for.

  • Pull fan-out sub-queries with high impressions and near-zero clicks, then cluster them into topics.
  • Baseline how often the brand is named, and which sources get cited instead, across tracked prompts.
  • Write against the clusters where citation rate is high but brand naming is low, since those are answers the engines already want and the brand is missing from.
  • Publish in waves, then re-baseline each wave to measure days to first tracked citation.
