AI Visibility Check

Enter a domain and see why AI search leaves it out. Reads the homepage, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml the way a crawler does, then reports which checks pass, which need fixing, and which could not be verified. Unverified checks are excluded from the score rather than guessed.

Not Slop

An embeddable badge that publicly claims a human is accountable for a page, paired with a reader-facing report button. Reports route to the publisher by email or webhook. Soulcraft never receives them. One line of script, MIT licensed.
