How It Works

Five steps to AI search visibility.

AI search optimization is the practice of getting a brand named and cited when people ask an AI assistant a question instead of typing into a search box. It spans three overlapping disciplines: SEO for ranked results, answer engine optimization (AEO) for direct answers, and generative engine optimization (GEO) for systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. We call the whole thing Superintelligence Engine Optimization, or simply SEO.

It is a method, not a mystery. Here is what we do, in order, on every engagement.

  1. Map the identity

    We build your soul file: voice, values, positioning, audience, competitive context. Every later step reads from this file, which is what keeps the output sounding like you instead of like the category.

  2. Baseline the answers

    We ask the engines the questions your buyers actually ask, then record what comes back: whether you are named, which sources get cited, and how much of the answer belongs to competitors. That baseline is what every later claim gets measured against.

  3. Make the site legible to machines

    Schema markup, a published entity map, clean heading structure, descriptive alt text. An answer engine cannot cite what it cannot parse, and most sites fail here before they fail on content.

  4. Publish the answers

    We write the pages that respond directly to those questions, in your voice, with definitions and comparisons up front where models extract them. Depth beats volume: one page that answers a question completely does more than ten that circle it.

  5. Earn citations, then measure the lift

    We pursue the sources these engines already trust, then re-run the baseline. You see citation lift, days to first tracked citation, and share of voice against the competitors you named, with the method shown next to the numbers.

Questions

What people ask us.

What is the soul file? The soul file is a structured document that captures your company's identity: voice, values, positioning, audience, and competitive context. It's the file that drives every piece of work Soulcraft produces, and the brief our AI tools read before they draft a word. Every page, every campaign, every optimization decision flows from this single source of truth.

How is Soulcraft different from a traditional marketing agency? Traditional agencies charge for a team's hours and route your work through account managers, strategists and junior writers. Soulcraft is a practitioner-led agency: the person you talk to is the person doing the work, with twenty years of experience and AI tools that let one expert do what used to take a department. We don't produce deliverables and hand them off. We build an identity-first program that evolves alongside your business.

How does Soulcraft use AI? As a tool, not a replacement. AI accelerates research, drafting, auditing and monitoring. A human sets strategy, writes and edits the final work, checks every claim, and is accountable for the result. Nothing publishes without a person signing off on it.

How much does Soulcraft cost? The core engagement is five thousand dollars per month. That covers a content engine and hub, AI visibility and share of voice monitoring, technical SEO auditing and website advisory, weekly reporting and strategy, weekly calls with Slack in between, and ideation on PR and thought leadership. Two add-ons run twenty five hundred dollars per month each and can be added or dropped month to month. The Distribution Engine tailors and posts each piece across LinkedIn, Medium, Reddit, Substack, and X. The Citation Engine handles outreach to Wikipedia, directories, and industry publications. No long term contracts. No hidden fees. No overhead passed to you.

How is AI search different from traditional search? Traditional search returns a ranked list of links and lets the person choose. AI search returns one composed answer and names a few sources inside it. That changes the goal: you are no longer competing for position three, you are competing to be the source the model quotes. A page can rank well on Google and still be invisible inside ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, because those systems select for parseable structure and clear, citable claims, not for links alone.

How long does it take to show up in AI answers? Technical legibility moves fastest: schema, entity data, and crawler access can be re-crawled within days. Content and citations take longer, usually one to three months before a new page is reliably pulled into answers, because the engines need to encounter it in enough places to trust it. Soulcraft tracks days to first tracked citation on every engagement, so the timeline is a measured number rather than a promise.

Which AI platforms does Soulcraft optimize for? ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, alongside traditional Google and Bing results. Soulcraft monitors each one separately, because they cite different sources and disagree more often than people expect. A brand can hold strong share of voice in Gemini and be absent from ChatGPT on the same question.

How do you measure AI search visibility? Three numbers, tracked from day one: citation lift across AI engines, days to first tracked citation, and share of voice against the competitors you name. Soulcraft runs the same prompt set on a fixed schedule so the comparison stays honest, and publishes the method alongside the results. Soulcraft does not report estimates. See how we measure AI visibility.

Does AI search replace SEO, or do we need both? They are the same discipline with a new finish line. The technical foundations overlap almost entirely: clean structure, fast pages, accurate schema, and real authority help in both places. What changed is what you measure. Traditional SEO measured rankings and clicks; today the work also has to earn mentions, citations, and recommendations inside answers that may never produce a click. Teams that drop the fundamentals to chase AI visibility usually lose the crawl quality that AI visibility depends on. We call the unified practice Superintelligence Engine Optimization, and keep the acronym: SEO.
