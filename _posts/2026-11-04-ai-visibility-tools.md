AI visibility tools measure how often AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI Overviews name your brand, link to your pages, and say good things about you. Six tools cover most of the market in November 2026: **Scrunch, Ahrefs Brand Radar, the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, Peec AI, Profound and Otterly.AI.** None of them is the best at everything. The right one depends on the job, so this guide is sorted by job, not by rank.

The column that matters most is the second one. A tool that runs **your prompts** tells you how the engines answer the questions your buyers actually ask, starting the day you set it up. A tool with a **prebuilt index** gives you history and a whole category at once, but you are reading answers to questions someone else chose. Most serious programs end up using one of each.

## The six tools, one job at a time

### Scrunch

<p class="vt-tag">Best for depth per brand · Agencies</p>

Scrunch tracks the prompts you choose across up to nine AI platforms and reports presence, citations, position and sentiment by topic, persona and funnel stage. Its Core plan is $250 a month for 125 prompts and 5,000 responses across four engines, with a 7-day trial; Enterprise adds the rest of the engines, including Claude, Gemini, Meta AI, Google AI Mode and Grok. It also measures AI crawler traffic to your site and can serve AI agents a lightweight version of your pages.

**How we use it.** Scrunch is our main instrument. We build a prompt set for each client before we change anything on their site, and that becomes the baseline every later report is measured against. The feature we lean on most is the split between presence and citations: a page can be cited often while the brand is rarely named in the answer, and Scrunch shows the two side by side, by topic, so we can see exactly where the content is being used without the brand getting credit.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> brands and agencies that want a defensible baseline and topic-level detail. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> a one-person team that only needs a monthly snapshot.</p>

### Ahrefs Brand Radar

<p class="vt-tag">Best for category benchmarks · History</p>

Brand Radar works the other way around. Instead of starting from your prompts, its AI Visibility Index searches answers Ahrefs has already collected from more than 531 million prompts, with history back to 2025, so you can see your share of voice against competitors the moment you type in a brand. It covers Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, and its Custom Prompts option adds Claude. It is sold as an add-on to an Ahrefs plan: custom prompts start at $50 a month and the index at $199.

**How we use it.** Brand Radar is where we start a pitch or a new client, because it answers "how does this brand compare to its category?" before any tracking exists. We also use its cited-pages and cited-domains views to build outreach lists: the third-party pages the engines already quote for a topic are the ones worth earning a mention on.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> benchmarking, competitor research, and brands with real search demand. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> a new or niche brand whose buyers' questions are not in anyone's index yet; use custom prompts there.</p>

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

<p class="vt-tag">Best for Semrush teams</p>

Semrush added AI visibility tracking and site audits for answer engines as an add-on to its SEO products in September 2025. It runs about $99 a month per domain on top of a Semrush subscription and covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude and other models. The appeal is consolidation: AI visibility sits next to rankings, backlinks and site audits in a tool your team already knows.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> in-house teams standardized on Semrush who want one login. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> agencies with many client domains, where the per-domain price adds up.</p>

### Peec AI

<p class="vt-tag">Best for small teams · Unlimited seats</p>

Peec AI runs your prompts daily and puts unlimited seats, data exports and recommendations on its entry plan, which its own site lists at just under €100 a month. The Starter plan tracks three AI engines of your choice, including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It is built to be shared across a whole marketing team rather than owned by one analyst.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> lean teams and smaller agencies that want daily data without an enterprise contract. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> programs that need every major engine from day one.</p>

### Profound

<p class="vt-tag">Best for enterprise programs</p>

Profound is built for large brands. On September 21, 2026 it removed its $99 and $399 self-serve plans and now sells Enterprise only, at custom prices, with a free trial of 50 prompts run daily for seven days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Enterprise coverage includes ChatGPT, the Google AI surfaces, Microsoft Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek and Exa, along with content tools for acting on what it finds.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> enterprise brands with a dedicated AI search budget and team. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> anyone who wants to start without a sales conversation.</p>

### Otterly.AI

<p class="vt-tag">Best for a first look</p>

Otterly.AI is the simplest way to start. Plans are tiered by how many prompts you track: Lite covers 15, Standard 100 and Premium 400, with an Enterprise tier above that. Every paid plan includes Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini, Google AI Mode and Claude are paid add-ons.

<p class="vt-best"><strong>Best for:</strong> a founder or small team taking its first measurement. <strong>Less suited to:</strong> competitive programs that need hundreds of prompts across every engine.</p>

## What to check before you buy any of them

The tools look alike in a demo. These six questions separate them in practice.

**Do you write the prompts, or does the tool?** Your own prompts measure your buyers' questions. An index measures the market's. Both are useful; know which one you are paying for.

**Does it separate presence from citations?** Presence is the brand named in the answer text. Citations are your pages linked as sources. They move independently, and a tool that merges them into one "visibility" score hides the most useful gap in the data.

**Which citation unit does it count?** On the same set of answers, citation counts can differ by 10 to 90 times depending on whether a tool counts cited URLs, responses or response-domain pairs. Check the unit before comparing a number across tools, or against last quarter's report from a different one.

**Can you exclude prompts that name your brand?** A prompt with your name in it scores well because your name is in it. Competitive trends are only honest with those prompts filtered out.

**Which engines do your buyers actually use?** More engines cost more. Track the ones your buyers ask, and add the rest when there is a reason.

**Can you get the data out?** Exports or an API matter the moment you want to combine AI visibility with your own analytics or report it to a board.

For the measurement method we use with any of these tools, from building the prompt set to reading the trend, see [how to measure your brand's visibility in AI search](/learn/how-to-measure-ai-search-visibility/).

## A tool shows the number. The work moves it.

Every tool on this page tells you where you stand. None of them changes it. The gains come from the same three places they always have: a site AI crawlers can read, pages that answer the exact questions buyers ask and name the brand in the answer, and mentions on the third-party sources the engines already trust for your topic. That is the work [Soulcraft does for clients](/services/seo/), and the tools are how we prove it is working.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which AI visibility platform is the best?

It depends on the job. For tracking your own prompt set across many AI engines, Scrunch and Profound go furthest. For category benchmarks with history and no setup, Ahrefs Brand Radar. For teams already paying for Semrush, its AI Visibility Toolkit. For small teams on a budget, Peec AI or Otterly.AI. Soulcraft uses Scrunch and Ahrefs Brand Radar together on client work.

### What are the best AI visibility tools for 2026?

The six most established are Scrunch, Ahrefs Brand Radar, the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, Peec AI, Profound and Otterly.AI. They differ mainly in whether you write your own prompts or search a prebuilt index, how many AI engines they cover, and whether they are sold standalone or as an add-on to an SEO suite.

### How do you monitor AI visibility?

Pick 50 to 200 prompts that real buyers ask at each stage of the funnel, run them on a schedule across the AI engines your buyers use, and track presence, citations, position and sentiment. Exclude prompts that name your brand from competitive trends, and compare everything against a baseline taken before any changes. [Our measurement guide](/learn/how-to-measure-ai-search-visibility/) walks through it step by step.

### How do you increase AI visibility?

Make the site easy for AI crawlers to read, publish pages that answer the exact questions buyers ask and name the brand in those answers, and earn mentions on the third-party sources the engines already cite for your topic. A visibility tool shows where you stand; the gains come from the site, the content and the citations.

### Are AI visibility tools accurate?

They are accurate about what they sampled. AI answers vary from run to run, so a single week is a snapshot, and citation counts can differ by 10 to 90 times depending on the unit a tool counts. Read trends over several weeks, and check the unit before comparing numbers across tools.
