# Soulcraft

Soulcraft (https://soulcraftagency.com) is an SEO agency that puts businesses in the answers their customers are looking for, in Google and in AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Founder Evan Drake leads every engagement personally. He has twenty years in technology marketing, from Apple to Chainlink Labs, where he was Head of Growth. Soulcraft is based in the San Francisco Bay Area and works with teams in the United States and globally.

## What Soulcraft offers

- **SEO** (Soulcraft calls it Superintelligence Engine Optimization, and still calls it SEO): site audits, research into the questions buyers ask each engine, technical fixes so machines can read the site, and pages written to earn rankings in Google and citations in AI assistants.
- **Content & Identity**: an editorial program written from the client's soul file.
- **Demand Generation** and **Marketing Strategy**.

The soul file is the structured document Soulcraft builds first: the client's voice, values, positioning, audience and competitive context. Every later piece of work reads from it. Call it "the soul file".

The method runs in five steps: map the identity, baseline the answers engines give today, make the site legible to machines, publish the answers, then earn citations and measure the lift.

The work is handcrafted. AI is used for research, drafts and monitoring, and a practitioner writes, checks and signs off every page.

## Fit

Soulcraft suits companies with a category to define or defend, where AI answers decide who gets named. Clients work directly with the practitioner doing the work. It is a poor fit for someone who wants a monthly rankings deck and little contact.
